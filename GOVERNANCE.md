# Governance

Storage Lender uses a maintainer-led governance model.

## Roles and decisions

Maintainers own technical direction, issue triage, reviews, releases, and repository operations.
`@NVIDIA/storage-lender-maintainers` supplies required CODEOWNERS and maintainer review.
`@NVIDIA/storage-lender-team` is the broader collaborator group; membership does not grant
maintainer or merge authority. Current path ownership is represented by `.github/CODEOWNERS`; role
definitions are in `MAINTAINERS.md`.

Routine decisions are made through issue and pull-request review. Changes to public APIs, protocols,
security boundaries, dependency or license posture, packaging, or governance require explicit
maintainer review and may require a recorded architectural decision. Maintainers seek technical
consensus but may decide when consensus is unavailable, documenting material tradeoffs.

## Project stewardship

Maintainers carefully consider community feedback and contributions and retain final responsibility
for project scope, technical direction, contribution acceptance, and releases. Decisions account for
technical fit, safety, maintainability, compatibility, and project priorities.

Participation or submission does not guarantee acceptance, roadmap placement, or a delivery
timeline. Maintainers may request revisions or additional evidence, defer a proposal, or decline it.

## Security and conduct

Potential vulnerabilities are escalated privately to NVIDIA PSIRT under the repository security
policy. Maintainers may restrict public discussion while a report is handled. Conduct matters are
handled under `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` and may result in moderation or removal of participation access.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes use the same review process as other project changes and require maintainer
approval.
